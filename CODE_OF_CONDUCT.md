# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. No harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, spam or publication of another person's private information. Critique
technical work without personal attacks. Respect requests to end an interaction.

The repository owner may remove abusive content, restrict participation or ban
repeat offenders, with proportionate reasons recorded privately. Report conduct
concerns privately to [info@zentarisystems.io](mailto:info@zentarisystems.io),
using the subject **Zentari Voice conduct report**. The repository owner
receives and reviews these reports. Include only information needed to assess
the incident; do not post confidential reports in public issues.

Appeals should be reviewed by a different maintainer when one is available.
No independent appeals reviewer or guaranteed response time is currently
promised. Delivery verification follows [the maintainer procedure](docs/MAINTAINER.md).
