# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive and specific. No harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, spam or publication of another person's private information. Critique
technical work without personal attacks. Respect requests to end an interaction.

The repository owner may remove abusive content, restrict participation or ban
repeat offenders, with proportionate reasons recorded privately. Appeals should
be reviewed by a different maintainer when one is available. Reports involving
private details require a working private contact; the owner must designate and
test that contact before public community launch. Do not publish a confidential
conduct report in an issue. Until that channel is established, community launch
remains gated and no confidential reporting service is promised.
