# Governance

The repository owner controls the initial preview and license decisions. Product
scope is local voice/text companionship; privileged tools, fleet management and
automatic paid fallback are outside this preview.

Decisions affecting protocol compatibility, data retention, security boundaries,
licensing and supported hardware need a written rationale and reviewable evidence.
Security-sensitive work needs a reviewer independent of its author. A second
release-capable maintainer, account recovery and a private vulnerability intake
must be established before stable launch. This document does not invent those
assignments or claim anyone has accepted on-call duty.

Brand and trademark rights are separate from the Apache source license. Do not
imply a fork is endorsed by Zentari. No additional restriction is placed on the
rights granted by the source license.
